Mascots

Lab mascot · the sky

Crane Hạc

In Vietnamese temples the crane (hạc) stands on the back of a turtle as a symbol of long life and grace. Our crane wears the lab's blue dress with the PAIRS emblem on the chest and a lotus at the hem. It stands for our aerial robots, which explore and map the world from above.

Lab mascot · the field

Buffalo Trâu

The water buffalo (trâu) has worked Vietnam's rice fields for centuries and is a symbol of hard work and patience. Our buffalo wears a checked scarf and a blue vest with the PAIRS emblem, and carries a conical hat (nón lá). It stands for our mobile robots, which work on the ground, step by step.
