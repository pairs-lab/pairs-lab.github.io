Faculty

Thanh Nguyen Canh

Lecturer, Robotics Department, VNU-UET

Thanh Nguyen Canh is a lecturer in the Robotics Department at the University of Engineering and Technology, Vietnam National University (VNU-UET), and a Ph.D. candidate at the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST), working on 3D active semantic SLAM, robot perception, navigation and learning.
