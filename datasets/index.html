02 / Hardware

Robot Platforms & Computing

Robots 5

Aerial robot

Custom Quadrotor UAV

Lab-built quadrotor with a 3D-printed frame, an onboard NVIDIA Jetson computer and an Intel RealSense depth camera, used for semantic mapping, active SLAM and autonomous exploration.

Mobile robot

TurtleBot 4

ROS 2 mobile robot on the iRobot Create 3 base with a 2D LiDAR and an OAK-D stereo camera, used for indoor navigation, mapping and social navigation experiments.

Collaborative arm

Universal Robots UR3

Six-axis collaborative arm for tabletop manipulation, grasping in clutter and human–robot collaboration.

Mobile robot

Raspberry Pi Mouse

Compact two-wheeled robot fitted with a 2D laser scanner and a webcam, used for teaching and for SLAM and localization experiments.

Service robot

Dual-Arm Service Robot

Mobile dual-arm robot designed and built in the lab for human–robot interaction, gesture and voice interaction, and service tasks.
