Meet our mascots

Lab Mascots

The crane, the buffalo and the golden carp move along the bottom of every page. Their mood follows how full, rested and happy they are: click one to feed, pet, nap or ride, or hide them with the crane button in the top bar.

Crane Hạc

In Vietnamese temples the crane (hạc) stands on the back of a turtle as a symbol of long life and grace. Our crane wears the lab's blue dress with the PAIRS emblem on the chest and a lotus at the hem. It stands for our aerial robots, which explore and map the world from above.

Buffalo Trâu

The water buffalo (trâu) has worked Vietnam's rice fields for centuries and is a symbol of hard work and patience. Our buffalo wears a checked scarf and a blue vest with the PAIRS emblem, and carries a conical hat (nón lá). It stands for our mobile robots, which work on the ground, step by step.

Golden carp Cá chép

In Vietnamese legend the carp (cá chép) swims upstream and leaps the Dragon Gate to become a dragon, a symbol of perseverance and success in study. Our golden carp wears the lab's blue dress with the PAIRS emblem and waves on the hem. It stands for our underwater robots, which explore where the crane and the buffalo cannot go.

Minigame

Landmark Relay

The crane flies a drone and drops map landmarks; the buffalo catches them on the ground, like an aerial and a ground robot mapping together. Catch blue landmarks (+1) and gold loop closures (+5), and let red outliers fall (−2 if caught).
